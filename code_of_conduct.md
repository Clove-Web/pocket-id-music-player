# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone who participates in this project.

Regardless of experience, background, identity, or skill level, everyone should be able to contribute in an environment free from harassment, discrimination, and unnecessary hostility.

## Expected Behaviour

Community members are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate.
* Be constructive when providing feedback.
* Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Help create a positive and collaborative environment.
* Focus discussions on improving the project.
* Assume good intentions while communicating respectfully.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include, but are not limited to:

* Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
* Personal attacks or abusive language.
* Trolling or intentionally disruptive behaviour.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Deliberately misleading contributors or users.
* Any conduct that would reasonably make others feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to:

**[admin@doughmination.win](mailto:admin@doughmination.win)**

Please include as much information as possible to help us understand and investigate the situation.

All reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Responses may include:

* A private warning.
* Temporary removal of comments or content.
* Temporary suspension from participation.
* Permanent removal from the project's community.

Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith and with the goal of maintaining a respectful environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* Repository discussions.
* Issues.
* Pull requests.
* Project-related communication.
* Any other official community spaces associated with this project.
